Drum kit: every key plays its own drum, written on the key. Kick on C, snare on D, hi-hats on the black keys. Pick one of 30 kits from the Kit menu under Controllers. The sound knobs don't change the kit; Tempo, the arpeggiator, Echo & space and Volume still work.

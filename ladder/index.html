Drum kit: every key plays its own drum, written on the key. Kick on C, snare on D, hi-hats on the black keys. Pick one of 30 kits from the Kit menu under Controllers. The sound knobs don't change the kit; Tempo, the arpeggiator, Echo & space and Volume still work.

Click a step to switch it on or off. Drag a step up or down (or use the arrow keys) to change its note. A plays it louder, S slides into the next step.

Click a square to add a hit; click again for a louder hit, and once more to clear it. Sixteen steps make one bar.

Press and move inside the pad: left to right is pitch, bottom to top is volume. It plays the sound that is loaded; try Etherwave Theremin in Moog Classics.

2 · Edit the notes

Draw on the grid to change the song. Rows that belong to the song's key are lit up, and with In key on, anything you draw lands on a note that fits.

Develop my idea reads the notes you drew, then rewrites this part across the whole song from them — repeating your idea, answering it, turning it upside down. It is how what you play teaches the generator.

3 · Shape the mix

Re-roll rewrites just that one part and keeps everything else. Lock a part (🔓) to protect it — locked parts survive a re-roll of everything.

4 · Take it with you

The WAV is the finished track. The MIDI holds every note on separate tracks, so you can open it in GarageBand, Ableton, FL Studio, Logic or MuseScore and change anything.

How this works

SONG FORGE composes music the way a songwriter does, one decision at a time. It picks a key and a scale, writes a chord progression, then builds a bassline, a drum groove, an arpeggio and a melody that all fit those chords — and arranges them into an intro, verses, a chorus, a bridge and an outro.

Every instrument is synthesised live in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is downloaded, and it works with the internet switched off.

Seeds

Every song has a seed — a short code like VELVET-7318. The same seed with the same settings always produces the same song, so Copy link gives someone the exact track you are hearing.

Tips

  • If one part bothers you, hit Re-roll on just that row.
  • Draw your own notes in Edit the notes, then press Develop my idea and the generator builds the song around what you wrote.
  • Lock a part you like so re-rolling everything leaves it alone.
  • Mute the drums for a version you can talk over.
  • Download the MIDI to keep writing in real music software.

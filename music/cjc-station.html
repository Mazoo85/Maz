Snare, clap, tom and the synth feed the sends. Delay is tempo-synced ping-pong.

How to play. Hit Play (or Space) — a groove is already loaded. Click an amber pad to cycle it off → on → accent (accents hit harder); click the teal grid to place bass notes (one per column). Everything is synthesized live with the Web Audio API — no samples, no files.
CJC Music Station — the browser edition of the native C++/SDL3 DAW. Your pattern is saved in this browser.

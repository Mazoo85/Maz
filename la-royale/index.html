How to play

MoveW A S D or arrow keys (left thumb stick on a phone)
LookMouse (click the game first) or drag on the right of the screen
SprintShift
JumpSpace: jump, leave the blimp, open your parachute
CarE to get in or out. W/S to drive, A/D to steer, Space to brake. A lowrider hops on Space. F turns on a police car's siren. H sounds the horn.
MapM opens the map. Tap anywhere on it, then "Drop here" to skydive there.
TimeT jumps the clock forward three hours. LA by night is worth it.
CameraMouse wheel zooms; C swaps near and far
M map · E car · T time · Space jump
Los Angeles Tap the map to choose where to drop
